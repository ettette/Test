Connecting Volunteers and Native Communities

We are a student-led organization based in the Bay Area that partners with Native communities to build projects around what they want and need. Through tutoring, STEM, engineering, technology programs, and other community-led projects, we bring student volunteers and communities together to provide practical support based on local needs.
