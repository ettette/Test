About Indigenous Futures Initiative

We bring together student volunteers to support Native communities through tutoring, STEM, engineering, and other projects based on community needs.

Our story

Why we started

Indigenous Futures Initiative is a student-led organization based in the Bay Area that works with Native communities through education, STEM, engineering, technology, and community-led projects.

We work directly with tribes, tribal education departments, and Native organizations to understand where student support could be useful. Each community can decide what type of support would be most helpful, and we will build the project around that.

When a community partner has specific requirements or onboarding, we will follow their process.

How we work

Listen before planning

Community partners know their students, priorities, and context. We begin with their perspective.

Be honest about capacity

We only offer projects our volunteers have the skills and time to support responsibly.

Begin with something manageable

A focused workshop or short tutoring pilot gives everyone room to learn what works.
