Programs and support

Tutoring, STEM, and academic support

The support we can offer depends on community priorities, volunteer skills, location, timing, and the requirements of each organization.

Tutoring and academic support

Direct learning support matched to subjects our volunteers can teach responsibly.

  • Homework help
  • Math, science, and physics
  • English and writing, when available
  • Individual or small-group tutoring
  • Remote tutoring
  • In-person tutoring where possible

STEM and engineering

Technical subjects made approachable through guidance, projects, and hands-on learning.

  • Introductory engineering workshops
  • Coding and computer science
  • Math and physics support
  • Electronics or technology projects
  • Robotics or CAD, when skills allow
  • Engineering career exposure

Test preparation

SAT, ACT, AP, and other test preparation support based on what students need.

  • SAT preparation
  • Practice questions, concept review, and test strategies
  • Academic mentoring
  • STEM and engineering pathways

Other community projects

Tutoring and STEM are our focus, but we may help with other projects that fit volunteer skills.

  • Basic website or digital help
  • Technology assistance
  • Youth activities and events
  • Environmental projects
  • Research and resource support
  • Other focused student-led projects

A note on scope

Examples are not guaranteed programs.

Activities such as robotics, electronics, coding, or CAD are offered only when we have qualified volunteers and when a community identifies them as useful.
