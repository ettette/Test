Working with communities

Working with tribes and community organizations

We work directly with communities to understand what support would be most useful. Each community can decide what type of project they want to create, and we will build the project around that.

Our role

Partnering directly with tribal communities

We already have students interested in volunteering and want to work with communities to create projects that are actually useful. This could include tutoring, STEM, engineering, test preparation, or another area where support is needed.

A first project might be weekly remote math tutoring, a STEM workshop, SAT preparation, an engineering activity day, college application help, or a focused technology project.

If your organization requires cultural competency learning, orientation, background checks, or another onboarding process, we are willing to follow those requirements.

What the process can look like

  1. 1

    You identify a need

    Tell us about the students you serve and an area where additional support might help.

  2. 2

    We talk through the details

    We decide whether our volunteer skills and availability are a good fit.

  3. 3

    We plan a manageable project

    Together, we choose a format, timeline, and clear expectations.

  4. 4

    We prepare volunteers

    We match relevant skills and complete requested orientation.

  5. 5

    We begin and adjust

    We run the project, listen to feedback, and improve before doing more.
