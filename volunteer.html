Student volunteering

Volunteer with our group

We are looking for students who can offer patient, reliable academic or technical support and who are willing to prepare carefully for each project.

Who can join

Students with skills they can share

You do not need to know everything. You do need to be honest about what you can teach, communicate reliably, prepare for your role, and respect the direction of the community organization we are working with.

Projects may take place remotely or in person. Opportunities will depend on current community needs, volunteer availability, location, and subject fit.

Before a project begins

Volunteer preparation

Depending on the community partner and the role, volunteers may need to complete orientation, cultural competency learning, background requirements, safety guidance, or other onboarding requested by that organization.

We will explain what is required before volunteers commit to a specific project.
